\section{Making Polarization Maps}
\label{sec: chap4_polarization_vectors}
The polarization morphology of a disk is represented by polarization vectors calculated from \SQ and $U$, which define the polarization angle (Equation \ref{eq: PA}). From these morphologies, the dominant polarization mechanism can be inferred, as different mechanisms produce different polarization patterns (Chapter \ref{sec: intro_polarization_mechanisms}). By comparing the polarization morphologies at multiple wavelengths, we can examine how the dominant polarization mechanism changes with optical depth. We first calculated the polarization vector and then created models to fit the observed morphologies and determine the dominant mechanisms.
