\subsection{Measuring Observed \POLF}
\label{sec: measured_POLF}
% ----------------------------------------------
A single polarization fraction value was obtained at each wavelength to compare to the dust scattering models. The models should be compared to the observations by taking the value in with evidence of self-scattering (uniform minor axis alignment). Three methods were tested to determine the best value:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The polarization fraction at the pixel with maximum \SI.
    \item The polarization fraction at the pixel with maximum polarized intensity.
    \item The mean polarization fraction within the uniform area from the Gaussian model ($W_{\text{uniform}}$= 1 $\pm$ 0.001, Equation \ref{eq: gaussian_eq}). 
\end{enumerate}
