\subsubsection{\draft{Dust Composition }}
% \label{sec: measured_POLF}

The dust grains are assumed to follow the Disk Substructures at High Angular Resolution Project (DSHARP) dust composition, a mixture of water ice, astronomical silicates, troilite and refractory
organic material \citep{dsharp_code}. The corresponding volume fractions, mass fractions, and sources for the refractive indices are listed in Table \ref{tab:dsharp_composition}.
