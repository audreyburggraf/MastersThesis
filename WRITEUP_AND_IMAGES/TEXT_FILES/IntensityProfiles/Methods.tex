
As a way to study the disk, we look at the intensity profile. To do this we take a slice along the minor axis of the disk in each band. The minor axis is at an angle of 90$^\circ$ from the position angle, which is stated for each wavelength in Table \ref{tab: source_info}. These slices were taken in both Stokes I and POLI. The slice is shown for each wavelength in Figure \ref{fig: slice-along-minor}. It should be notes that these slices are shown for POLI, but the exact same slice was taken in Stokes I. It was done in both Stokes I and POLI to allow us to compare any differences between the slices when looking at all light, versus just the polarized light.
