Figure \ref{fig: polarization_transition} highlights the transition in polarization morphology with wavelength. At higher wavelengths, the polarization morphology is predominantly azimuthal (circular), with very few vectors aligned with the minor axis of the disk. As the wavelength decreases to the right in the grid, the area in the centre that is aligned with the minor axis of the disk becomes more prominent, and the azimuthal pattern is on the outer parts of the disk.
