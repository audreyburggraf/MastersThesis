\section{Additional Spherical Grain Models \POLFsi}
\label{sec: appendix_maxStokesI}


Additional plots with results comparing \Pwmodel curves and \POLFsi are presented below. Figure \ref{fig: Spherical_BEST}, presented in Chapter \ref{sec: chapter5_spherical_dust_model_results}, shows the best-fit results for the all wavelengths fitting case for filling factors of $f = 1.0, 0.5, 0.3$ and $0.1$. Figure \ref{fig: appendix_without_b5_ALL} shows the results when \bfive is excluded from the fit, while Figure \ref{fig: appendix_just_47_ALL} shows the results when only \bfour and \bseven are included. Figures \ref{fig: appendix_without_b5_ALL} and \ref{fig: appendix_just_47_ALL} include results for all filling factors.
