\section{Stokes I}
% ------------------------------------------------
In Figure \ref{fig: slices_StokesI} we present plots of the \stokesI values along the slices along the minor axis for all 4 bands. When we look at the slices in Stokes I, we can see that the shapes are all centred at the origin. Meaning when we look at all of the light from the disk (polarized or not), we see the most light at the centre of the disk. This is expected, as the <YSO> is at the centre of the disk, so it should be brightest there.

\section{POLI}
% ------------------------------------------------
In Figure \ref{fig: slices_StokesI} we present plots of the POLI values along the slices along the minor axis for all 4 bands. When we look at the slices of POLI, we see some features that are different than \stokesI. Primarily that the curves are no longer centred at (0,0), and that they all have a slight shift to the right. In simpler terms, when we look at the polarized light, it no longer peaks at the centre of the disk, it is peaking at some point in the disk that is <closer or farther towards us>. 

This can give us some insight into the structure of the disk. <flared, source>.
