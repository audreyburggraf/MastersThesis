\section{Making Polarization Maps}
\label{sec: chap4_polarization_vectors}
The polarization morphology of a disk is represented by polarization vectors found from \SQ and $U$, which define the polarization angle (Equation \ref{eq: PA}). From these morphologies, the dominant polarization mechanism can be inferred, as different mechanisms produce different patterns (Chapter \ref{sec: intro_polarization_mechanisms}). By comparing the polarization morphologies at multiple wavelengths, we can see how the dominant polarization mechanism changes with optical depth. We started by calculating the polarization vectors, and later created models to fit the morphologies to determine the dominant mechanisms.
