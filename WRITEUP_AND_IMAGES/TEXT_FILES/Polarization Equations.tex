\subsection{Polarized Fraction}
The linear polarization fraction,
\begin{equation}
    P = \sqrt{Q^2+U^2}/I,
    \label{eq: POLF}
\end{equation}
\noindent where $I$, $Q$ and $U$ are Stokes parameters
