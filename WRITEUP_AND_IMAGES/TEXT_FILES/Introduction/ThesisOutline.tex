This thesis is organized into seven chapters. Chapter 2 introduces the basics of radio interferometry, including how ALMA collects data and processes observational data. Chapter 3 introduces the multi-wavelength polarization observations and polarization quantities used throughout the project. Chapter 4 presents the observational analysis, including the creation and analysis of polarization maps in \SI and polarized intensity, as well as radial intensity profiles. Chapter 5 looks at modelling the polarization, including geometric models of the polarization morphology and dust scattering models using both spherical and irregular-shaped grains. Chapter 6 discusses the implications of the results for the polarization mechanisms and dust properties of IRS 63. Finally, Chapter 7 summarizes the main conclusions of the project.
