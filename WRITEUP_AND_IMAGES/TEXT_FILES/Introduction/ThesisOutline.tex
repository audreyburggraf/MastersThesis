This thesis is organized into seven chapters. Chapter 2 provides an introduction to the basics of radio interferometers, how ALMA collects data and how it is processed. Chapter 3 introduces the multiwavelength polarized emission data and polarization quantities. Chapter 4 provides information on the observational analysis, including making and analyzing polarization maps in \SI and polarized intensity, and intensity profiles. Chapter 5 looks at modelling the polarization, including geometric modelling of the polarization morphologies and dust scattering models using both spherical and irregular-shaped grains.
