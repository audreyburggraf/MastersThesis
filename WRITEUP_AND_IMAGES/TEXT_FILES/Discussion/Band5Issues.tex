

% \textbf{Initial Analysis.} 
The observations at \bfive were initially imaged with \texttt{robust = 0.5}, the same as the imaging parameters for the other wavelengths. However, during the dust-scattering modelling for spherical grains, the \POLFSI value of 0.99 was found to be lower than expected based on the polarization fractions at the other wavelengths, and negatively affected the success of the models. This motivated us to determine if different \rob parameters could affect the polarization fraction at \bfiveNS.






% \textbf{Imaging Differences.} 
The \rob parameter was decreased from 0.5 (\RfNS) to -1 (\RoNS), shifting the Briggs weighting closer to uniform weighting. This increased angular resolution, with the beam size decreasing from $0.33'' \times 0.27''$ to $0.23'' \times 0.18''$. The higher angular resolution comes with the trade-off of lower sensitivity, with the \SIerr increasing from 13.88 to  42.12 $\mu$Jy. Due to the lower sensitivity, fewer polarization vectors are detected, and the streamer is no longer detected (Chapter \ref{sec: streamer}). Figure \ref{fig: Band5_robust05} presents the \SI and debiased polarized intensity maps overlaid with the polarization vectors for \RfNS. This is the equivalent of Figure \ref{fig: StokesI_POLI_grid} for \RoNS.

% \textbf{Polarization Morphology.} 
Despite the change in beam size and number of detected polarization vectors, the overall polarization morphology remains similar between the two weighting schemes. The polarization vectors have similar orientations, indicating that the observed morphology is not strongly dependent on the image weighting. The main difference is the number of detected vectors, which is expected to be the result of the change in sensitivity. 







% \textbf{Polarization Fraction and Modelling.} 
Although the polarization morphology remains similar, the \POLFsi value between the images changes. The \POLFsi value increased from 0.99 to 1.40, which agrees more with the polarization fractions measured at the other wavelengths. This shows that the choice of the \rob parameter can affect the measured polarization fraction even when the overall polarization morphology remains similar. The \Ro image and its associated polarization fraction value were therefore used for the analysis of the dust-scattering models, as its value is more consistent with the other wavelengths.
