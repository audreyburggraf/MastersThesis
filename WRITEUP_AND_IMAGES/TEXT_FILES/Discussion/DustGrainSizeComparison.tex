Previous studies of other disks have found a similarly constrained grain size distribution. 

For example, HL Tau. 
\cite{Kataoka2016} constrained the dust grain size using polarization observations for HL Tau at \bsixNS, and found a maximum grain size of $\sim$ 150 \micronNS. 
This work was extended by \cite{Yang2016} to include the polarization induced by disc inclination with respect to the line of sight, and they found a value of 72 \micronNS. 
\cite{Carrasco} studied the same disk using thermal emission and found particles on the order of a few mm at the centre, which decreases with radius (1 mm at 100 au). 

\cite{Mori} studied the disk with polarization observations again but at a higher wavelength of 3.1 mm and found a maximum grain size of $\sim$ 130. 


\cite{Ueda} used polarization emission at \beven and found the maximum dust grain size of $\sim$100 \micronNS, while the spectral energy distribution suggested the maximum dust grain size is approximately a millimetre.
