These results show that for all four cases, the lowest $\chi_\mathcal{P}^2$ occurs for a filling factor of $f = 0.5$. The compact-grain case of ($f = 1.0$) produces a higher $\chi_\mathcal{P}^2$ value in all fitting cases, while the more porous models ($f=0.3$ and $f=0.1$) provide worse fits than $f = 0.5$. This suggests that a slightly porous grain model is the best agreement with the observed polarization fraction.
