\subsubsection{Scattering Opacities}
To begin the spherical dust modeling, one of the first steps was to ensure that I could recreate figures that have been make previously. In Figure \ref{fig: Kataoka2015Figure1Recreation}, I present my recreation of Figure 1 from \cite{Kataoka_2015}. In this figure we show the absorption and scattering opacities in units of \cmsq per gram as a function of wavelength for a maximum graim sizes of 1 \micron and 100 \micron. 

We can see that at a smaller dust grain (red lines, \amax = 1 \micronNS), the absoprtion opacity is much greater than the scattering opacity. 

For the larger dust grain (blue lines, \amax = 100 \micronNS), from 0.1-1 mm ($10^2 - 10^3$\micronNS), the scattering efficiency is larger than absorption.

\subsubsection{Polarization \& Scattering Angle}
% ----------------------------------------------
In Figure \ref{fig: Kataoka2015Figure2Recreation}, I replicated Figure 2 from \cite{Kataoka_2015} for the four bands I am looking at. In these plots we look at how polarization changes with scattering angle. Here, we plot \quoted{the degree of polarization due to single scatting averaged by the size distribution for several maximum grain sizes} \cite{Kataoka_2015}. $N_{11}$ is calculated in Equation \ref{eq: N11}. 

We see that when we are looking at smaller grains with a size of 10 \micron and 100 \micron the polarization degree peaks at a scatting angle of $\theta$ = 90\deggNS.

\subsection{Fit Results}
% \label{sec: measured_POLF}
% ----------------------------------------------
In Table \ref{tab:dust_model_fits_spherical} we report the scale factor, chi-squared and maximum dust grain size for 4 values of posisity, with multiple fit types. 


We can see that across all of the fits, the best fit (lowest chi-squared) is the fit with a porosity of $f$ = 0.75 when we include all bands. However, we also note that the results comparing all bands versus Band 5 not included are essentially the exact same. 

In Figure \ref{fig: DustModels_Spherical_WithoutBand5}, we show the jplots associated with the fit without Band 5. The equivalent plots for the other fits can be found in Appendix \ref{chap: Appendix_DustModels}. In this figure, the observed \POLF value for each band is plotted as a colour-matching marker, the values of these are listed in Table \ref{tab:polf_comparison}, in the POLF$_{\text{max \SI}}$ column. 

This figure highlights how the Band 5 marker (yellow triangle) is significantly lower than the model predicts. However, the robust = -1 Band 5 marker (green plus sign)is a much better match.
