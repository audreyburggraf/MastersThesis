\subsubsection{Challenges and Solution }

Up to this point, in the field, majority of dust grain modelling has used the assumption of spherical grains. Models with non-spherical grains are more realistic to the actual dust grains in a disk, however they are associated with additinal complications and a lot of computation power needed. 

Non-spherical dust grain modeling has become more accessable usiong the code called \texttt{glitterin} from \cite{glitterin}.
